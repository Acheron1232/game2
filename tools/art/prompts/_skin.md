These are TEXTURES for a low-poly 3D game character, painted in the style of classic early-2000s fantasy RTS model textures: bold hand-painted light and shadow already in the paint (light from the top), strong dark outlines between shapes, rich saturated colors, crisp clear details that read when small. Not photo-realistic, not a drawing of a whole character. Every cell is a flat texture swatch seen straight on, filling the WHOLE cell edge to edge, with no background, no frame, no empty space and no drop shadow. Team color parts are pure magenta (#FF00FF) with painted shading and must not appear anywhere else.
