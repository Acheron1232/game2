# Terrain meadow v2 — one test sheet, six seamless ground textures

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production diffuse ground textures for an original low-poly fantasy RTS game, viewed from a distant top-down camera.

Generate ONE landscape image arranged as a strict 3-column by 2-row grid of equal square texture cells. Fill every cell completely. Separate cells with thin straight dark grey lines. No labels or text. Each cell is independently seamless and tileable, flat orthographic top-down, no perspective, no horizon, no vignette or directional lighting. Soft hand-painted medium-sized color masses, restrained small details, matte albedo, no baked shadows or sculpted relief. Legible quiet ground underneath small heroes; nothing should look like raised giant leaves, tufts, needles, woven fibers, a lawn photograph or a high-contrast carpet.

Row 1 left to right:

1. Meadow grass: short close-cut soft grass blades, muted sage and olive green, occasional tiny brown earth flecks. Low contrast and gently varied organic color patches. No flowers or clover.
2. Lush meadow grass: same detail size and style, subtly richer cool green, softly varied moss and tiny short blades, no large leaves or strong motifs.
3. Dry grass: same surface, restrained faded olive and straw, brown soil visible between short sparse blades. No yellow glare or hay piles.

Row 2 left to right: 4. Worn earth path: warm neutral umber and dusty brown, subtle worn mottling, a few tiny subdued pebbles. No orange tint, grooves or large rocks. 5. Rough earth: slightly darker neutral brown, small gravel and soft earth clods, understated contrast, compatible with the path. 6. Forest floor: deep olive-brown earth and moss, a few small dry leaf fragments and thin twigs. No recognizable repeating leaf cluster, no black patches.

Consistent restrained painted fantasy art style and color family across all six tiles. Every tile must look good repeated 4 by 4 at 256 pixels per tile. No dramatic highlights, sharp black outlines, objects, buildings, characters, symbols or watermark.
